Chess Square Color

Can you tell if a square is white or black just from its name?

How to solve it:
Convert the file (a=1, b=2 ... h=8) to a number.
If both file and rank are odd or both even — it is Black.
If they are different (one odd, one even) — it is White.

Example: c4 — c=3 (odd), 4 (even) — White
