About AD Chess

Our Mission
AD Chess Academy is dedicated to teaching chess in a fun, structured, and engaging way — from complete beginners to competitive players. Our games reinforce classroom concepts so students can practice anytime, anywhere.
Why Chess?
Chess builds critical thinking, concentration, and problem-solving skills that benefit students in every area of life. We make learning chess enjoyable and accessible for all ages.
